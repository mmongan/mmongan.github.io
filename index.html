Drill form editor

Create standing forms without replacing the show. Select a form here or select one of its marchers. Moves, rotations, and scaling apply to the whole form; collision checks reject overlapping edits. Selection, shapes, and sets

Close Settings to select on the field. Drag a rectangle, Shift-drag a lasso, Ctrl/Cmd-click to toggle a marcher, or Ctrl/Cmd+A to select all eligible marchers. Cyan circles show selection; orange and green handles rotate and scale it. Drag selected marchers to move the selection. Desktop selection gestures replace camera dragging only while enabled.

Set edits change selected marchers at the destination set and linearly rebuild their transitions to adjacent sets. Other marchers and distant counts remain unchanged. Existing midsets within those transitions are replaced. Destination collisions are rejected; inspect collision previews and stride warnings during playback. Changes are session-local, with no show export or edit undo.

Choose a destination set above. Chart mode pauses the real band at that set, adds cyan ghosts at the previous set, and draws gold arrows from old to new positions. Holds have no arrow. Arrows show straight-line displacement, not the traveled route. The first set has no previous-set comparison. Playback, timeline jogging, or ghost-band preview turns chart mode off. Turning it off leaves the band paused; no drill positions are edited.

Transforms, alignment, locks, and rounding use the shared form-editor settings above, but act on this selection.

Drafting table

Draft on a tabletop-sized field placed in front of you. Toggle Full-size view for a live, view-only field in the distance, including marchers and guides; editing stays on the table. Exiting restores the previous field placement. No pins. Endpoint and line snaps take priority over nearby grid points.

Select a guide to configure its marcher spacing (0.625-10 yards) and facing: along, reverse, or a fixed field angle (0 degrees toward +Z; 90 toward +X). For exact spacing, enter Divider spacing below and apply it to the guide. Landing ghosts and Create formation use the same spaced positions, starting at the guide's first point.

Moves use Divider spacing / parallel offset below. Rotations use the set-square angle around the guide's bounding center. Duplicates are independent copies shifted +X by one move step. Out-of-field edits are rejected. Undo guide removes the newest whole guide, not individual edits.

Choose a local image to project and trace.

Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP (up to 16 MB) before entering XR. Images stay in this browser session and are resized to the device texture limit (at most 4096 pixels per side). The image is clipped to the field, keeps its aspect ratio, and does not intercept drawing. At zero rotation the image top points toward +Z. Trace it with drawing tools, then Create formation/path as usual. Projector on the floating settings panel opens alignment, scale, rotation, fade, and on/off controls. Moving the projector body only repositions its controls. Put away hides the device but keeps the projection. Calibration temporarily hides the image. Reload/Remove clears only the image; no image or projection settings are saved in drafting workspaces/camera photos, and no automatic image-to-route conversion occurs.

The camera saves one top-down PNG and its drafting workspace together, separately from Save drafting locally. Restore camera snapshot restores drafting only, not marchers or routes. Download field photo saves an image only; it cannot restore drafting.
