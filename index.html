Drill form editor

Create standing forms without replacing the show. Select a form here or select one of its marchers. Moves, rotations, and scaling apply to the whole form; collision checks reject overlapping edits.

Hold Shift to temporarily bypass grid and angle snapping. Press 1 outside text fields to round the selected standing form to whole yards.

Align X makes a line along Z; align Z makes a line along X. Distribution keeps the outermost positions and spaces the others evenly on that axis. Colliding results are rejected.

Selection, shapes, and sets

Close Settings to select on the field. Drag a rectangle, Shift-drag a lasso, Ctrl/Cmd-click to toggle a marcher, or Ctrl/Cmd+A to select all eligible marchers. Cyan circles show selection; orange and green handles rotate and scale it. Drag selected marchers to move the selection. Desktop selection gestures replace camera dragging only while enabled.

Set edits change selected marchers at the destination set and linearly rebuild their transitions to adjacent sets. Other marchers and distant counts remain unchanged. Existing midsets within those transitions are replaced. Destination collisions are rejected; inspect collision previews and stride warnings during playback. Changes are session-local, with no show export or edit undo.

Transforms, alignment, locks, and rounding use the shared form-editor settings above, but act on this selection.

Shape inspector

Selection order assigns marchers along the shape. Start a shape, append segments, then apply coordinates or group. Move starts a disconnected section; Close returns to the first point. Blue field handles edit endpoints and curve controls. Applying never creates or deletes marchers.
